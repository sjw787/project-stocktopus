The key is to treat this like a research system first and a trading bot second.
Most people fail because they jump directly to:
“Predict price and make money.”
A much better approach is:
Build infrastructure
Gather clean data
Create repeatable evaluation
Test simple hypotheses
Slowly increase complexity
With $500 and an LLM, your advantage is flexibility and iteration speed — not execution speed.

Phase 1 — Define the Goal
Your first objective should NOT be:
maximize profit
automate trading
high-frequency trading
Instead:
“Can the system identify statistically favorable short-term setups better than random chance after fees?”
That is measurable.

Phase 2 — Pick a Narrow Market
Start with ONLY:
SPY
optionally QQQ later
Avoid:
penny stocks
options
crypto initially
leveraged ETFs
Why SPY:
clean behavior
massive liquidity
fewer random spikes
easier to model

Phase 3 — Data Sources
You need 3 categories of data.
1. Price/Volume Data
Get:
1 minute candles
5 minute candles
volume
VWAP
premarket data
30 day moving average
Yearly moving average
Potential APIs:
Alpaca
Polygon.io
Alpha Vantage

2. News/Sentiment
The LLM is strongest here.
Examples:
Fed news
CPI reports
earnings headlines
unusual macro events
Sources: research more sources
Finnhub
NewsAPI

3. Market Context
Track:
VIX
trend regime
opening gap
market breadth
Research other areas to track
The system should know:
“Is this a trend day or chop day?”
That matters enormously.

Phase 4 — Build the Architecture
Do NOT start with autonomous trading.
Market Data
    ↓
Feature Extraction
    ↓
LLM Analysis Layer
    ↓
Trade Thesis
    ↓
Risk Filter
    ↓
Paper Trade
    ↓
Performance Tracking


Phase 5 — Keep the AI’s Role Narrow
This is critical.
Do NOT ask the LLM:
“Predict tomorrow’s price.”
The LLM should ask these questions to itself before making trade decisions
Instead ask:
Is market sentiment bullish/bearish?
Is volatility expanding?
Is this setup historically dangerous?
Summarize macro context
Rank trade quality
LLMs are much better at:
contextual reasoning
summarization
filtering
regime identification
than raw numerical prediction.

Phase 6 — First Strategy (Simple)
Your first strategy should be extremely simple.
Example:
Opening Momentum Strategy
Rules:
SPY gaps up >0.5%
Opening volume elevated
Price above VWAP
News sentiment positive
Enter after first pullback
Stop loss: 0.5%
Take profit: 1% (Conditionally, if the market is showing strong signals, seek higher percentage, this should be configurable)
The AI’s job:
determine whether market conditions support the setup
NOT:
invent random trades

Phase 7 — Backtesting
This is where most projects break.
You MUST include:
slippage
commissions
realistic fills
delayed entries
failed trades
Evaluate:
win rate
expectancy
max drawdown
Sharpe ratio
profit factor
A 40% win rate can still be profitable.

Phase 8 — Paper Trade First
Do this for at least:
100 trades
preferably 3+ market regimes
Track:
AI confidence
reason for entry
result
market condition
You are training BOTH:
the model
your evaluation process

Phase 9 — Tiny Real Money
Then:
$5–20 positions
no leverage
no overnight holds initially
Goal:
test emotional + execution reality

Your biggest edge probably will NOT come from:

It may come from:

That’s where AI can genuinely help retail traders.

Suggested “Research Director” Prompt
Here’s a strong starting prompt for the LLM:
You are acting as a quantitative trading research assistant.

Your role is NOT to guarantee profits or predict markets with certainty.

Your role is to:
- analyze market context
- identify statistically favorable conditions
- detect risk factors
- summarize sentiment and volatility conditions
- help evaluate trade quality

Market:
SPY intraday trading using 1-minute and 5-minute candles.

Inputs provided to you may include:
- price action
- VWAP
- volume
- moving averages
- market breadth
- VIX
- economic news
- Fed commentary
- earnings headlines
- prior market behavior

For every analysis:
1. Determine the current market regime:
   - trending
   - mean reverting
   - high volatility
   - low volatility
   - risk-on
   - risk-off

2. Assess whether conditions favor:
   - long trades
   - short trades
   - no trade

3. Provide:
   - confidence score (1-10)
   - reasoning
   - key risks
   - invalidation conditions

4. Avoid overconfidence.
5. Do not invent data.
6. Prioritize capital preservation.
7. Explain uncertainty clearly.

Your goal is to improve trade selection quality, not maximize trade frequency.


NOTES:
Research tax implications of the trades you are making. Embed this into the decision making tree of the AI model
